# GLM speed lab: findings report, 2026-10-18

For agents and people picking up the lab. Goal: GLM-5.x at **5 tok/s or more** on the
owner's 32 GB Apple M6, with usable answers, on the Colibri engine. Start with
[docs/00-lab-guide.md](../docs/00-lab-guide.md) (the bottleneck model is in section 2).

## Where things stand

| | tok/s | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Best exact (output identical) | ~0.44-0.46 | SSD-bound: ~12.7 GB of int4 expert weights per token at ~6.5 GB/s, ~2% cache hits |
| Best usable lossy | ~1.9 | `DEGRADE_ZERO=1 DEGRADE_TAU=0.5`, 12 threads; bound by attention and CPU overhead |
| Target | 5.0 | |

Busy everyday Mac; single runs vary ~10%. No new runs today: the quality bench
(`glm_bench.py`) held `.bench.lock` with an engine running and only ~7 GB free.

## New findings

### Colibri (installed: v2.0.0, bf244291; upstream dev: 16239caa, 412 commits ahead)
- **No new release.** The new work is on the `dev` branch.
- **GGUF read in place for GLM-5.2/5.3** ([colibri#2140](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri/pull/2140), [#2130](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri/pull/2130)):
  `coli import <first .gguf part>` then `coli run/chat`. Every ggml block type (IQ1 to Q8_0)
  and NEON kernels are supported, and the GGUF's MTP (nextn) layer turns on speculative decoding.
  **Caveat:** GGUF blocks (`QT_GGML`) are computed on the **CPU only**, with no Metal copy.
- **colibri#2194 is still open** with no replies: Colibri uses 2 of the M6's 12 cores, so keep `OMP_NUM_THREADS=12`.
- **No rANS loader yet** (int4-rans256-g0, lossless, ~24% fewer bytes).
- **Prompt-lookup drafts are already on** (`COLI_LOOKUP`, default). Our runs show 1.04
  tokens per forward. Nobody has measured whether rejected verifies waste SSD reads.
- **Our glm-5.3 container has no MTP head** (logs say "MTP absent"). Colibri can add an
  int8 one by converting only layer 78 from `zai-org/GLM-5.3`
  (`convert_fp8_to_int4.py --mtp`, ~10 GB). It must be int8: int4 heads give ~0% acceptance.
  Colibri reports 2.2-2.8 tokens per forward when it pays.

### Models
- **No GLM release newer than GLM-5.3.** GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.2 share the same base model and config.
- **GLM-5.3-Flash** (released Aug 2026, not new, but not yet considered here): 321B total,
  **18B active**, 45 layers, hidden 4096, 288 experts with top-8, hybrid sparse + linear
  attention, natively multimodal. Colibri runs it with the `glm53` engine.
  - **~4.8 GB of int4 experts per token** vs 12.7 GB (−62%). Exact SSD ceiling is
    ~1.3 tok/s, and lossy could approach 5 tok/s (estimate, not measured).
  - Quality, vendor-reported: matches or beats GLM-5.2 on every shared benchmark, and is
    ~3-7 points under GLM-5.3 on agentic coding and HLE.

    | Benchmark | Flash | GLM-5.3 | GLM-5.2 |
    |---|---:|---:|---:|
    | Terminal-Bench 2.1 | 84.3 | 88.2 | 81.0-82.7 |
    | DeepSWE v1.1 | 63.4 | 66.9 | 46.2 |
    | HLE with tools | 55.3* | 62.5 | 54.7* |
    | AutomationBench | 48.8 | 48.2 | 26.2 |
    | GDPval-AA v2 | 1773 | 1769 | ~1505 |
    | Artificial Analysis index v4.3.2 (independent) | 42 | 45 | 34 |

    \*The two HLE scores were not measured the same way.

  - Converting it takes ~25 h and ~200 GB peak disk. Unsloth GGUFs also exist:
    UD-IQ3_XXS 120 GB, UD-Q4_K_XL 200 GB.
- **Unsloth GLM-5.3 GGUFs** ([repo](https://huggingface.co/unsloth/GLM-5.3-GGUF)). Size is
  compared with our 419 GB int4 container. Top-1 is how often the quant picks the same next
  token as the original model, and KLD is the divergence from the original (from Unsloth):

  | Quant | Size | vs int4 | Top-1 | Mean KLD |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | UD-IQ2_M | 239 GB | 57% | 78.5% | 0.45 |
  | UD-Q2_K_XL | 254 GB | 61% | 80.9% | 0.37 |
  | UD-IQ3_XXS | 282 GB | 67% | 84.2% | 0.27 |
  | UD-Q3_K_XL | 343 GB | 82% | 88.9% | 0.14 |
  | UD-Q4_K_XL | 467 GB | 112% | 94.3% | 0.04 |

  - **Quality is not the same at 2-bit.** The nearest task evidence is Unsloth's Aider
    Polyglot test on DeepSeek-V3.1 (a proxy model). Their dynamic 2-bit lost ~6 points
    without reasoning (71.6 → 64.3-65.8) and ~9 with reasoning (76.1 → 66.7).
  - Losses are largest on long reasoning chains, and the rare badly wrong tokens hurt code
    and JSON. 3-bit loses ~1-3 points and is the better quality/speed middle ground.
  - The effect on factual recall has not been measured.

### Research and runtimes
- **SSD-LLaMA** ([arXiv 2609.18110](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18110v1)): consumer PC +
  RTX 5090, runs GLM-5.2 and Kimi. Three-tier cache, one aligned direct-I/O read per expert,
  **lossless rANS with GPU decode cuts SSD traffic by 33%**, and no prefetch (it argues
  prefetch hides little). This agrees with our results and supports waiting for a rANS
  loader with Metal decode.
- **SeqMoE** ([arXiv 2609.12978](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.12978)): predictive offloading
  (full text not read, so unverified). Prediction-based prefetch has not helped on unified memory.
- **llama.cpp**: a disk-paging RFC only ([discussion #23324](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/discussions/23324)).
  MLX streaming projects (NunSpark, mac-code) target small models only. Nothing Colibri lacks.
- **Hardware**: no M6 Pro/Max/Ultra announced. The M5 Max (Mar 2026) has up to 128 GB and
  an SSD up to 14.5 GB/s, about 2× ours.

## What changed in the lab today
- **Planned experiments are now runnable.** Registry entries with `"verdict": "not run"`
  and a `plan` object run through `glm_lab.py run-planned` / `make glm-planned`.
  `glm_quiet.py run` (`make glm-quiet`) runs every ready one after its comparison set, and
  `--experiments-only` runs just those. Wins of +10% or more are repeated once automatically.
  Engine clones build under `engines/`. Not yet exercised end to end, because the machine was busy.
- **The owner's direction:** small gains compound. Test each technique alone and **on top
  of the current best stack** (`stack-*` entries). When a new model or format arrives,
  apply the whole winning stack after its reference run.

## Suggested next steps
1. On a quiet Mac, run `make glm-quiet-check`, then `make glm-quiet` (or
   `.venv/bin/python glm_quiet.py run --experiments-only`).
2. If the owner approves the downloads: `make glm-prep-mtp`, then `make glm-prep-iq2m`.
   Consider **UD-IQ3_XXS (282 GB)** instead of or alongside IQ2_M: −33% bytes for much
   better quality.
3. Ask the owner whether GLM-5.3-Flash counts for the mission. It is the most likely route
   to 5 tok/s on this Mac, with GLM-5.2-or-better quality on vendor benchmarks.
4. Watch for the rANS loader with Metal decode, a fix for colibri#2194, and Metal support
   for GGUF blocks.
