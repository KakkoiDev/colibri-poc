Does GLM-5.3 stay correct when we make it faster?

24 graded questions (facts, maths, logic, code checked by running tests, and explanations), answered by GLM-5.3 on a 32 GB Apple M6 with each speed setting and two prompt styles. Every answer is below.

Test conditions. Measured on the owner's busy everyday Mac (other apps open, sometimes a large download). Answer times may be better on a quiet machine. "Skip" settings change the model's output; "exact" gives the model's own answer. Single run per question and setting.

Results by setting

Settings: exact = the model's own computation; skip X = experts that are not in RAM and weigh less than X are skipped instead of read from the SSD (faster, changes output). Terse prompts end with "Answer as briefly as possible. For code, reply with only the code." Normal answers were capped at 128 tokens.
