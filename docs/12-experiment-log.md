Generated by `glm_lab.py log` from `experiments/glm-experiments.json` and the published runs; do not edit by hand.

> Measured on a busy everyday machine with other apps open; single runs unless noted, so changes under ~10% are noise.

- **Overlap SSD reads with GPU work** (`pipe`): change `PIPE=1`. Why: Reads and math happen one after the other; overlapping should hide some read time. Result: Small gain within noise on its own; kept because it combines with DIRECT. Reference: `c2-metal-r18-c1k`.
- **Uncached SSD reads** (`direct`): change `PIPE=1 DIRECT=1`. Why: Reading around the page cache avoids a copy and enables zero-copy GPU buffers. Result: Faster; Colibri reports DIRECT can be drive-dependent. Reference: `c3-pipe`.
- **4 SSD readers instead of 8** (`readers-4`): change `PIPE_WORKERS=4`. Why: Fewer readers might reduce contention. Result: Within noise of 8 readers; the SSD is the limit, not the reader count. Reference: `c4-pipe-direct`.
- **2 SSD readers** (`readers-2`): change `PIPE_WORKERS=2`. Why: iobench saturates at 2 readers. Result: No change. Reference: `c9-best-combo`.
- **16 SSD readers** (`readers-16`): change `PIPE_WORKERS=16`. Why: More queue depth might help the SSD. Result: No change. Reference: `c9-best-combo`.
- **Cross-layer expert prefetch** (`pilot`): change `PILOT=1 PILOT_REAL=1`. Why: Loading the next layer's likely experts early should hide read time. Result: Hit rate rose to 10% but speed did not; most prefetched loads were discarded. Reference: `c4-pipe-direct`.
- **Second SSD for expert reads** (`ssd-mirror`): change `COLI_MODEL_MIRROR / COLI_MODEL_DIRS`. Why: Exact runs are SSD-bound; splitting reads across two drives could nearly double read speed. Result: Not run: needs an external NVMe drive.
